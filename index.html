About

One person,
many worlds.

Sophia Blair is a multidimensional media creative who refuses to be put in a box — and that's exactly the point. Her work lives at the intersection of storytelling, visual culture, and human connection. From the pages of editorial shoots to the emotional depths of a scene, from the intimacy of an interview to the energy of a music set — Sophia brings a singular perspective to everything she touches.

Based at the crossroads of film, fashion, social media, and music, she is as comfortable in front of a camera as behind it, as fluent in long-form narrative as she is in the language of the scroll.
